## Human Review

AI outputs should be reviewed before they drive sensitive document actions, external communications, legal decisions, compliance workflows or business-record updates.

## Future Integration Notes

Workflow recommendations, Medha-driven reasoning across document workflows and MedhaOS-governed AI actions are described in this repository as future integration areas, not as generally available SastraPDF private-alpha capabilities.
