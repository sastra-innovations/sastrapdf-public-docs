# Summarization and Question Answering

SastraPDF can provide AI-assisted summaries and document-grounded question answering for permitted document context. These outputs are drafts for review, not authoritative decisions.

## Capabilities

| Capability | Status | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Summarization | Implemented; validation pending | Produces concise summaries from document text or OCR. |
| Document-grounded question answering | Implemented; validation pending | Answers questions using permitted document context. |

## Review Boundary

Users should review summaries and answers before relying on them for legal, compliance, financial, operational or customer-facing decisions. The product does not claim error-free responses or complete answers.

## Medha Relationship

Medha owns reasoning, retrieval, context and model selection when integrated. SastraPDF supplies document context and performs document operations. MedhaOS governs approved model paths and policy controls where enabled.
